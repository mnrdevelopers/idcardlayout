1:1 Physical Print Scale Checklist

Important for Physical Dimension Accuracy:

Standard printer dialogs often default to "Fit to Printable Area", which shrinks cards by 3-5%. To ensure your card prints at exactly 85.60 × 53.98 mm:

  • Destination Paper: A4 (210 × 297 mm)
  • Scale: 100% (Actual Size / Do not scale)
  • Margins: None or Default / Zero
  • Double-sided / Duplex: Off (Single sided specimen)

Always verify the printed card with a physical ruler before cutting.
